$$ Fable 2 Guild chest - WinDbg breakpoint script
$$ Run with:  $$><D:\...\Fable 2 Rexglue\tools\chest_debug.txt
$$ Then F5, boot into the game, click the Guild chest.
$$
$$ Recompiled guest fns are emitted as __imp__<name> (the real body).
$$ PPCContext is passed in rcx. Guest GPR offsets:
$$   r3=+0x00  r4=+0x20  r5=+0x28  r31=+0xF8  lr=+0x100
$$ Every bp ends in gc (auto-continue). Remove the gc to BREAK instead.
$$ =============================================================================
